The conversion Kit allows you to convert an existing Leo Rover 1.8 to version
1.9. This update brings significant improvements to computational performance,
mechanical robustness, and thermal management — extending the range of
environments and use cases where the rover can operate reliably.

### What’s Included

- New electronics box with improved heat dissipation
- Reinforced suspension components
- Upgraded computing unit with a more powerful CPU
- 12 MP Arducam camera
- Additional fasteners (screws, nuts, spacers) and updated cables

| Leo Rover 1.8                    | Leo Rover 1.9                                        |
