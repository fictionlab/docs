# High Capacity Battery Specification

---

High capacity battery is an optional battery replacement for the Leo Rover 1.6
or newer. It is a ~160 Wh lithium-ion battery, which translates to around 10
hours of driving or 20 hours of video streaming time.

Addon comes with a 5 A charger, which allows for a full charge in approximately
3 hours. With the standard charger (2 A), the charging time is about 8 hours.

:::note

Due to restrictions on battery transportation, standard shipping of this addon
is not possible outside inland Europe.

If you wish to order one, please contact us at contact@fictionlab.pl to discuss
shipping options.

:::

## User manual

### Charging the battery

To charge the battery **use only 12.6V chargers from Fictionlab**. It is
recommended to use the provided 5 A charger, which allows for a full charge in
approximately 3 hours.

:::tip

You can also use the standard Leo Rover 2 A charger, but the charging time will
be longer (approximately 8 hours).

:::

:::danger

Do not use any other chargers (especially rated higher than 12.6V), as it may
damage the battery or cause safety hazards.

Check the charger specifications before use. The charger must output 12.6 V with
a current limit of 5 A or lower. Using a charger with incorrect output voltage
or without proper current limiting may damage the battery or cause safety
hazards.

:::

1. Connect the charger to the provided Weipu to 5.5 mm barrel connector adapter
   cable.
2. Connect the Weipu connector to the battery pack.
3. Plug the charger into a power outlet.
4. Press the button on the battery. The LED indicator on the charger power
   supply will light up red, indicating that the battery is charging.
5. When the LED indicator on the charger power supply turns green, the battery
   is fully charged. Unplug the charger from the power outlet and disconnect the
   Weipu connector from the battery pack.

:::note

LED on the battery pack will not light up during charging.

:::

:::danger

Do not charge standard battery pack with 5 A charger from the high capacity
battery addon.

It can lead to overheating, damage to the battery pack and it may pose safety
hazards. Use only the provided 2 A charger for standard battery pack.

:::

### Mounting the battery

The high capacity battery is designed to be mounted as direct replacement for
the standard battery pack. It is compatible with Leo Rover version 1.6 or newer.
The mounting process is the same as for the standard battery pack. Please refer
to the
[Leo Rover assembly manual](/leo-rover/manuals/combining-subassemblies#step-3)
for detailed instructions on how to mount the battery.

### Maintenance and safety

- Do not disassemble the battery pack. It contains high voltage components that
  can cause electric shock or fire.
- Do not expose unmounted battery pack to water or moisture. When disconnected
  from the robot, the battery pack is not waterproof and can be damaged by water
  and other liquids.
- Do not expose the battery pack to high temperatures or prolonged direct
  sunlight. The battery pack can be damaged by heat and may pose a fire hazard.
- Store the battery pack in a cool, dry place when not in use. Avoid leaving it
  near heat sources.
- Do not short-circuit the battery pack. Avoid contact between the positive and
  negative terminals of the battery pack, as it can cause a short circuit and
  damage the battery or cause safety hazards.
- Do not puncture or crush the battery pack. Avoid dropping or applying
  excessive force to the battery pack, as it can cause internal damage and pose
  safety hazards.
- Avoid deep discharge of the battery pack as it will reduce its lifespan. It is
  recommended to recharge the battery pack when the voltage drops below 10 V.
- Do not use the battery pack if it is damaged, swollen, or leaking. If you
  notice any signs of damage, stop using the battery pack immediately and
  contact Fictionlab for support.
- Do not dispose of the battery pack in fire or water. Follow local regulations
  for proper disposal of lithium-ion batteries.
