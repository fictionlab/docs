### Hardware structure

<FlexTable className={styles.dark_block}>
  <FlexTableItem  style={{
      width: '50%'
    }}>

Raph Rover's architecture is simple and transparent to give you maximum control.
At the lowest level is the hardware layer (drives, sensors, battery). It is
managed by out custom controller - RaphCore and **UP7000** Single board computer
with **Ubuntu** and **ROS 2**. You work at the highest layer, creating standard
ROS nodes (in **Python** or **C++**)that implement your application's logic.
