## From Idea to Prototype: Common Challenges in Robotics

<FlexTable style={{ alignContent: 'center', justifyContent: 'space-evenly', alignItems: 'center'}}>
  <FlexTableItem
    style={{
      width: '35%'
    }}>
      <ImageZoom
        src="/img/robots/raph/overview/raph_street.webp"
        alt="Raph rover on the street - AI Background"
        width="1200"
        height="876"
        caption="*Render + AI background"
      />
  </FlexTableItem>
  <FlexTableItem
    style={{
      width: '55%'
    }}>

Building a platform from scratch takes months. Mechanical design, motor
selection, and debugging electronics **pull you away from your primary goal**:
developing innovative algorithms and software.

Then comes the integration problem. Your advanced LiDAR and camera sit on a
shelf because you **lack stable mounts and easy to use power connectors**. Every
new component becomes a frustrating hardware battle.

  </FlexTableItem>
</FlexTable>

<FlexTable style={{ alignContent: 'center', justifyContent: 'space-evenly', alignItems: 'center'}}>
  <FlexTableItem
    style={{
      width: '50%'
    }}>

Finally, there's software configuration - a steep learning curve and endless
driver issues **instead of a quick start with ROS 2** and immediate programming.

Beyond the initial build, there is the constant battle for reliability. Homemade
platforms are plagued by inconsistent performance, from loose connections to
motor failures that can ruin experiments and invalidate hours of data.

  </FlexTableItem>

    style={{

## Raph Rover: The Solution Engineered for You

<br />

<FlexTable>
  <FlexTableItem style={{
    width: '30%',
  }}>

**Ready to Go From Day One**

Instead of building, you're testing. Raph Rover is a fully assembled,
professional base with a 4x4 drivetrain and an 8-hour battery life. Save
valuable time and focus on what matters most.

  </FlexTableItem>
  <FlexTableItem style={{
    width: '30%',
  }}>

**Seamless Hardware Integration**

Forget makeshift solutions. The modular mounting plate (with an M4 grid) and
dedicated power outputs (5V, 12V, Vin) make adding your sensors and devices fast
and simple.

  </FlexTableItem>
  <FlexTableItem style={{
    width: '30%',
  }}>

**Native ROS 2 Environment**

Power up the robot and start coding. Pre-installed Ubuntu with ROS 2 you can
immediately leverage extensive documentation of ready-to-use packages by
connecting to the rover via Wi-Fi.

  </FlexTableItem>
</FlexTable>
