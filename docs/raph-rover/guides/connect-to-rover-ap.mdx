# How to Connect to the Rover's Access Point

To connect to Raph Rover, turn it on by holding the power button located on the
side of the rover for 1.5 seconds. The LEDs should light up green. After about
30 seconds the router should be operational and the access point should be up.

After the rover is powered on, you can connect to its Wi-Fi network. Default
credentials are:

```
SSID: Raph-XXXX-2G (or Raph-XXXX-5G for 5 GHz)
Password: fictionlab
```

:::note

The `XXXX` stands for a unique identifier specific to your Rover's computer.

:::
