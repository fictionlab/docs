To connect to Raph Rover, turn it on by holding the power button located on the
side of the rover for 3 seconds. The LEDs should start blinking white.
After about 30 seconds the LED should stop blinking and the Rover should be operational.

After the Rover is powered on, you can connect to its Wi-Fi network. Default
credentials are:

```
SSID: Raph-XXXX-2G (or Raph-XXXX-5G for 5 GHz)
Password: fictionlab
```

:::note

The `XXXX` stands for a unique identifier specific to your Rover's
computer. **You can find it on a plate located on the bottom of the Rover** -<font color="red"> TODO verify this. </font>

:::
