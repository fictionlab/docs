When you connect the Rover to your local network that provides Internet
connection, you'll be able to download files to the Rover, as well as forward
the Internet to your computer when you're connected to the Rover's access point.

Raph Rover comes with a router that can connect to a WiFi network as a client.

In order to do that, you need to access the router's web interface. Open a browser and
navigate to http://10.10.0.1. Use the credentials provided below to log in:

- **username:** `admin`
- **password:** `Fictionlab1`

## Connect to a WiFi network

Go to Network → Wireless → SSIDs tab.

<ImageZoom
  src="/img/robots/raph/guides/connect-to-network/rutx-ssids.webp"
  alt="RUTX SSIDs list"
  width="1920"
  height="991"
/>

Click `Scan 2.4 GHz` or `Scan 5 GHz` depending on the bandwidth of the network you want to connect to.

:::warning

Using the router in client mode will disable the access point functionality on the chosen band.
You will have to connect to the other band to access the Rover.

:::

Choose the network you want to connect to from the list and click `Join Network`.

<ImageZoom
  src="/img/robots/raph/guides/connect-to-network/rutx-scan-results.webp"
  alt="RUTX scan results"
  width="1535"
  height="797"
/>

Enter the WPA passphrase of the network and click `Submit`.

<ImageZoom
  src="/img/robots/raph/guides/connect-to-network/rutx-joining-network.webp"
  alt="Network joining window"
  width="1535"
  height="797"
/>

The settings of the connection will be shown. You can modify them according to your needs.
However, in most cases the default settings should work.
Click `Save & Apply` to connect to the network.

<ImageZoom
  src="/img/robots/raph/guides/connect-to-network/rutx-network-confirmation.webp"
  alt="Network confirmation window"
  width="1535"
  height="797"
/>

The router will take a few seconds to apply the changes and connect to the network.

:::success

You are now connected to the network. You can check the connection status in
Network → Wireless → SSIDs tab. You can now connect to the Raph Rover's access point
on the other band and access the Internet from your rover.
:::
