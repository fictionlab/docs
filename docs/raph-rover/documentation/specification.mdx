## Payload

The Raph Rover is engineered for easy and versatile payload integration. If your
sensor or hardware is ROS 2-compatible and falls within the rover's power (5V 2A,
12V 2A, and 24V 4A) and weight limits (10kg), you can be confident in its
compatibility.
