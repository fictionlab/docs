## Connecting batteries

Each Raph Rover is equipped with two dedicated 24V Li-Ion battery packs. As long
as at least one charged battery is plugged into the rover all of the
functionality is available. It is allowed to hot-swap batteries (replace
discharged ones with new ones without turning the rover off for the process).

To power the rover, insert at leas one battery pack into its designated slot
located on either side of the rover.

:::warning

Make sure that there is no debris inside the slots or in the battery connectors!

:::

The batteries are secured by ball catches, which provide slight resistance
during insertion and removal. A properly inserted battery should be fully nested
within the slot.

:::info

You should hear a **click** when the batteries are fully inserted.

:::

## Charging batteries

Raph Rover comes with a 4A 25,2V Li-Ion charger. It can charge 1 battery pack in
about 1.5 hour via the dedicated charging slot.

<ThemedImageZoom
  alt="Raph Rover battery dock"

The charger (not the dock) is outfitted with an LED status indicator designed to
provide information about the charging status of the battery. A green LED
signifies that the battery has reached full charge, while a red LED indicates
that the battery is currently in the charging process.

As an alternative, the batteries can be charged via the rover, by leaving them
inside the battery ports and connecting the charger to the rover's power input
located at the back.

This way it's possible to charge both batteries at the same time, however the
charging current will be split between them, resulting in a longer charging time
of around 2.5h Rover does not need to be powered off for this process.

:::note

When working on the rover while charging the batteries the LED indicator of
power adapter will not transition to green when charging is complete. Power draw
of the rover will keep the charger in active (red) state. To verify that the
batteries are fully charged, press the power button once.

:::

## Calibration

After each power-off, Raph Rover loses the position of its turning wheels,
requiring calibration. This process is initiated using the <font color="c07b2c">
**BACK** </font> button positioned at the back of the rover. Hold it for 3
seconds until the LED strips shines blue.

Wheels will begin to rotate inwards to locate the induction sensor, then bump
into the frame to set zero position. This 2-step process ensures that the wheels
are calibrated without obstruction.

## Web interface & first ride

Connect to the rover's Wi-Fi network to access its web interface using the
credentials provided below:

- **SSID 2.4 GHz:** `Raph-XXXX-2G`
- **SSID 5 GHz:** `Raph-XXXX-5G`
- **Password:** `fictionlab`

Open your web browser and navigate to http://10.10.0.2 <font color="red"> Good
IP? </font> to access the rover's control panel.

From here, you can control the rover, access the camera feed, and configure
various settings. You can control the rover using the on-screen joystick

<font color="red"> or by connecting a gamepad. </font>
