## Connecting batteries

Each Raph Rover is equipped with two dedicated 24V Li-Ion battery packs. As long
as at least one charged battery is plugged into the rover all of the
functionality is available. It is allowed to hot-swap batteries (replace
discharged ones with new ones without turning the rover off for the process).

To power the rover, insert at leas one battery pack into its designated slot
located on either side of the rover.

:::warning

Make sure that there is no debris inside the slots or in the battery connectors!

:::

The batteries are secured by ball catches, which provide slight resistance
during insertion and removal. A properly inserted battery should be fully nested
within the slot.

:::info

You should hear a **click** when the batteries are fully inserted.

:::

## Turning wheels calibration

After each power-off, Raph Rover loses the position of its turning wheels,
requiring calibration. This process is initiated using the  <font color="c07b2c"> **BACK** </font>  button
positioned at the back of the rover. Hold it for 3 seconds until the LED strips
shines blue. 

Wheels will begin to rotate inwards to locate the 
induction sensor, then bump into the frame to set zero position. 
This 2-step process ensures that the wheels are calibrated without obstruction.

### Connecting via SSH

:::note

The following names and passwords are intended solely for beta users.
Configuration settings will be updated for production-ready rovers.

:::

To connect to Raph Rover's computer via SSH, you must first establish a
connection with the on-board Wi-Fi network. The default credentials for the
access points are as follows:

- **SSID 2.4 GHz:** `Raph-XXXX-2G`
- **SSID 5 GHz:** `Raph-XXXX-5G`
- **Password:** `fictionlab`

:::note

`XXXX` is the rover's serial number

:::

Once connected to the network, you can access the on-board devices using the
following IP addresses and default credentials:

- **Router (RUTX10) :** `10.10.0.1`
- **Controller (RaphCore):** `10.10.0.2`
- **On-board computer (UpBoard 7000):** `10.10.0.3`

To connect to the UpBoard via SSH, use your preferred SSH client and the
following credentials:

- **user:** `raph`
- **password:** `raph`

To connect with the router, go to http://10.10.0.1 and login using credentials
below:

- **user:** `admin`
- **password:** `Fictionlab1`

## Battery charging

By default Raph Rover comes with 2A 25,2V Li-Ion charger. It can charge 1
battery pack in about 2,5 hours. Raph Rover has 2 options for charging
batteries:

- Outside the rover, by plugging battery into charger
- Inside the rover, by plugging charger directly into rear charging slot of the
  rover

The charger (not the dock) is outfitted with an LED status indicator designed to
provide information about the charging status of the battery. A green LED
signifies that the battery has reached full charge, while a red LED indicates
that the battery is currently in the charging process.

:::note

When charging the batteries with the rover powered on, the LED indicator will
not transition to green during the charging cycle. This is due to the fact that
the charger will be used to sustain the operational state of the rover after the
charging process.

:::
